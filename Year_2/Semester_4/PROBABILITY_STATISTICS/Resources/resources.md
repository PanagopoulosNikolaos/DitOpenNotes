# Probability and Statistics: Curated Resources and Reference Guide

A curated reference guide of authoritative textbooks, statistical programming platforms, and online interactive distributions for Probability and Statistics (Course Code 405).

---

## 1. Foundational Textbooks

- **Probability and Statistics for Engineers and Scientists**  
  *Authors:* Ronald E. Walpole, Raymond H. Myers, Sharon L. Myers, and Keying Ye.  
  *Annotation:* Comprehensive applied textbook emphasizing descriptive statistics, discrete and continuous random variables, Central Limit Theorem, confidence intervals, and hypothesis testing.

- **A First Course in Probability**  
  *Author:* Sheldon Ross.  
  *Annotation:* Rigorous mathematical exposition of Kolmogorov's axioms, combinatorial analysis, conditional probability, expectation, and limit theorems.

- **Introduction to Probability, Statistics, and Random Processes**  
  *Author:* Hossein Pishro-Nik.  
  *Annotation:* Outstanding open-access textbook connecting core probability models with engineering communications and computer science algorithms.

---

## 2. Statistical Computing Platforms and Tools

- **The R Project for Statistical Computing**  
  *URL:* `https://www.r-project.org/`  
  *Description:* Authoritative environment for statistical computing, data analysis, distribution modeling, and publication-grade graphics.

- **Seeing Theory: A Visual Introduction to Probability and Data**  
  *URL:* `https://seeing-theory.brown.edu/`  
  *Description:* Interactive visual simulations developed at Brown University demonstrating basic probability, conditional probability, random variables, and inference.

- **SciPy Statistical Functions (`scipy.stats`)**  
  *URL:* `https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/stats.html`  
  *Description:* Comprehensive Python scientific library containing probability distributions, summary statistics, and hypothesis tests.

---

## 3. Reference Standards

- **NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook of Statistical Methods**  
  *URL:* `https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/`  
  *Description:* Engineering handbook covering exploratory data analysis, distribution parameter estimation, and goodness-of-fit testing.
