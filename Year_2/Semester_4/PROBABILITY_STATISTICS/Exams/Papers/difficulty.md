## Detailed Breakdown of Ratings

### 1/5 - Easy
Papers at this level contain basic statistical and probability concepts with minimal algebraic manipulation:
*   **Descriptive Statistics:** Calculations are done on small, ungrouped datasets (typically 10-11 values).
*   **Probability:** Straightforward questions on independent or disjoint events and simple set operations (unions, intersections).
*   **Distributions:** Standard binomial and normal calculations where all parameters ($\mu, \sigma, n, p$) are directly provided.

### 2/5 - Lower-Intermediate
Papers at this level introduce grouped frequency tables and basic structural combinations:
*   **Descriptive Statistics:** Calculations (mean, median, standard deviation) are performed on grouped frequency tables (4-5 classes).
*   **Probability:** Basic applications of set theory and probability rules.
*   **Distributions:** Direct application of binomial and normal distribution formulas.

### 3/5 - Upper-Intermediate
Papers at this level require more conceptual understanding and probability derivations:
*   **Descriptive Statistics:** Calculating coefficient of variation ($CV$), variance, and quartiles from ungrouped data.
*   **Probability:** Proofs of event independence and complex conditional probability calculations (e.g., $P(A' \mid B')$).
*   **Distributions:** Standard binomial and normal distribution problems.

### 4/5 - Hard
Papers at this level require advanced algebraic manipulation and multi-step reasoning:
*   **Descriptive Statistics:** Grouped data tables with missing frequency values that must be solved first.
*   **Probability:** Comprehensive Bayes' theorem problems incorporating diagnostic test metrics (sensitivity, specificity, base rate).
*   **Distributions:**
    *   Binomial problems requiring solving inequalities with logarithms (e.g., finding minimum trials $n$ for a target probability).
    *   Normal distribution problems where one parameter (e.g., standard deviation $\sigma$) is unknown and must be derived from a percentile.

### 5/5 - Very Hard
The highest level of difficulty, combining advanced statistical formulas and complex systems of equations:
*   **Descriptive Statistics:** Calculating overall pooled mean and pooled variance for multiple combined datasets with different sizes and variances.
*   **Probability:** Complex binary transmission channel models with signal transmission and error rates requiring multiple Bayes' applications.
*   **Distributions:**
    *   Conditional binomial probability questions (e.g., $P(X = k \mid X \ge m)$).
    *   Normal distribution problems where both the mean $\mu$ and standard deviation $\sigma$ are unknown, requiring setting up and solving a system of two linear equations with two unknowns using percentiles and standard normal z-scores.
