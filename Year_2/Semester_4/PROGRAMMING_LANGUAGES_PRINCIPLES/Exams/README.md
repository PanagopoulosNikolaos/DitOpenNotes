## Recommended Exam Preparation Strategy

1. **Foundations**: Review syntax, grammars, and operational semantics in [`../Lectures/`](../Lectures/) and deep-dive language notes in [`../Resources/Notes/`](../Resources/Notes/).
2. **Coding Practice**: Solve the Haskell and Prolog problem sets in [`../Exercises/`](../Exercises/) and review the Python laboratory solutions in [`../Exercises/Lab/`](../Exercises/Lab/).
3. **Mock Exam**: Complete [`practice_exam_01.md`](practice_exam_01.md) under timed, closed-book conditions before reviewing past papers.
