# Recommended Resources: Discrete Mathematics

This document compiles curated textbooks, course notes, online proof visualization platforms, and algorithmic references for discrete mathematics in computer science.

---

## 1. Textbooks and Academic Monographs

* **Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications (8th Edition)**  
  *Author:* Kenneth H. Rosen  
  *Description:* The premier standard reference across computer science departments globally, providing extensive exercises in logic, set theory, combinatorics, probability, graph theory, and Boolean algebra.

* **Discrete Mathematics with Applications (5th Edition)**  
  *Author:* Susanna S. Epp  
  *Description:* Renowned for its clear pedagogical focus on mathematical proof construction, logic development, and recursive thinking.

* **Mathematics for Computer Science**  
  *Authors:* Eric Lehman, F. Thomson Leighton, Albert R. Meyer (MIT OpenCourseWare)  
  *Access:* [MIT OCW 6.042J](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-042j-mathematics-for-computer-science-spring-2015/)  
  *Description:* Outstanding free textbook and course materials focusing on formal logic, graph theory, asymptotic notation, and counting techniques.

* **Concrete Mathematics: A Foundation for Computer Science (2nd Edition)**  
  *Authors:* Ronald L. Graham, Donald E. Knuth, Oren Patashnik  
  *Description:* Rigorous mathematical text on discrete calculus, binomial coefficients, generating functions, and recurrence solving.

## 2. Interactive Environments and Online Tools

* **NetworkX (Python Network Analysis Library)**  
  *Documentation:* [https://networkx.org/](https://networkx.org/)  
  *Usage:* Powerful Python package for studying the structure, dynamics, and functions of complex networks and graphs.

* **Graphviz & Visualizing Graph Traversal**  
  *Website:* [https://graphviz.org/](https://graphviz.org/) / [Visualgo.net](https://visualgo.net/en/dfsbfs)  
  *Usage:* Automated layout and visual rendering of directed, undirected, and tree structures.

* **Truth Table Generator & SAT Solvers**  
  *Website:* [https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs103/truth-table-tool/](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs103/truth-table-tool/)  
  *Usage:* Interactive tool for verifying equivalences, evaluating complex propositional logic statements, and testing tautologies.
