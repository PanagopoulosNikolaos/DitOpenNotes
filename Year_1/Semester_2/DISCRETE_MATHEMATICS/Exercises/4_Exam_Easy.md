# **Discrete Mathematics Exam - Difficulty: EASY**



## **Topic 1. (2 points)** 
Construct the truth table of the following propositional formulas:

**a.** (1 point) $(p \land q) \to (p \lor q)$

**b.** (1 point) $\neg p \to (p \to q)$

***

## **Topic 2. (1 point)** 
In a survey of 100 participants, they were asked which of two colors (red, blue) they like. Of these:
- 60 answered that they like red
- 40 answered that they like blue  
- 20 answered that they like both

Calculate how many of the participants like neither of the two colors.

***

## **Topic 3. (1 point)** 
Consider the experiment of rolling two distinct fair six-sided dice (d6).

**a.** (0.5 points) Enumerate the possible outcomes of the form (even, odd).

**b.** (0.5 points) Enumerate the possible outcomes that sum to 7.

***

## **Topic 4. (1 point)** 
A disease appears in 5% of the population. A test has:
- 10% probability of a false negative result
- 5% probability of a false positive result

**a.** (0.5 points) Calculate the probability that a person has the disease and the test gives a negative result.

**b.** (0.5 points) Calculate the probability that the test gives a positive result.

***

## **Topic 5. (1 point)**
For the following relation on $S = \{1, 2, 3\}$, check the validity of the properties: reflexive, symmetric and transitive.

$R = \{(1,1), (2,2), (3,3), (1,2), (2,3)\}$

***

## **Topic 6. (2 points)**
Consider the following graphs:

$G_1 = (V_1 = \{A, B, C\}, E_1 = \{(A,B), (B,C), (A,C)\})$

$G_2 = (V_2 = \{1, 2, 3\}, E_2 = \{(1,2), (2,3), (1,3)\})$

**a.** (1 point) Show whether the graphs $G_1$ and $G_2$ are isomorphic.

**b.** (1 point) Calculate the degree of each vertex in the graph $G_1$.

***

## **Topic 7. (0.5 points)**
Write a regular expression that describes the set of strings over the alphabet $\{0,1\}$ that contain **exactly two** occurrences of 0.

***

## **Topic 8. (0.5 points)**
Write which of the strings cat, bat, hat, mat belong to the regular set described by the regular expression: $(c|b|h)at$

***

## **Topic 9. (1 point)**
Show by induction that for every $n \geq 1$ it holds:

$$1 + 2 + 3 + \ldots + n = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$$

***

## **Basic Clarifications:**

- **Truth table:** Use T for true and F for false
- **Regular expressions:** Use the notations $|$ (OR), $*$ (Kleene star), $+$ (one or more)
- **Induction:** Show the base case ($n=1$) and the inductive step
- Pencil is allowed for scrap work and blue or black pen for the final answer
