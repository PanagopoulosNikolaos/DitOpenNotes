# Recommended Resources: Digital Electronics

This document provides a curated list of academic textbooks, simulation tools, online interactive environments, and reference manuals for digital systems design and switching theory.

---

## 1. Textbooks and Reference Books

* **Digital Design: With an Introduction to the Verilog HDL, VHDL, and SystemVerilog (6th Edition)**  
  *Authors:* M. Morris R. Mano, Michael D. Ciletti  
  *Description:* The standard international textbook for undergraduate digital logic, covering Boolean algebra, combinational and sequential synthesis, registers, counters, and memory structures.

* **Fundamentals of Logic Design (7th Edition)**  
  *Authors:* Charles H. Roth Jr., Larry L. Kinney  
  *Description:* Exceptional coverage of algorithmic state machines (ASM), hazard analysis, K-maps, VHDL design, and FPGA implementation.

* **The Designer's Guide to VHDL (3rd Edition)**  
  *Author:* Peter J. Ashenden  
  *Description:* Authoritative reference manual for IEEE 1076 standard VHDL syntax, structural modeling, and verification techniques.

---

## 2. Open-Source Simulation and Synthesis Tooling

* **GHDL (Open-Source Analyzer, Compiler, and Simulator for VHDL)**  
  *Repository:* [https://github.com/ghdl/ghdl](https://github.com/ghdl/ghdl)  
  *Description:* Fast VHDL simulator translating VHDL code directly into machine code via LLVM/GCC backends.

* **GTKWave (Waveform Viewer)**  
  *Website:* [http://gtkwave.sourceforge.net/](http://gtkwave.sourceforge.net/)  
  *Description:* Interactive digital waveform viewer for analyzing `.vcd` and `.ghw` signal trace dumps generated during simulation.

* **EDA Playground**  
  *Website:* [https://www.edaplayground.com/](https://www.edaplayground.com/)  
  *Description:* Web-based IDE allowing simulation and synthesis of VHDL and Verilog designs in the cloud without local installations.

* **Falstad Circuit Simulator**  
  *Website:* [https://www.falstad.com/circuit/](https://www.falstad.com/circuit/)  
  *Description:* Interactive visual simulator for gate-level logic circuits, flip-flops, adders, and multi-vibrators.
