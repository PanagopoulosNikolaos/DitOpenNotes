# Recommended Resources: Fundamentals of Electromagnetics

This document provides a curated compilation of standard university textbooks, simulation software, open-courseware video series, and computational physics libraries for electromagnetic field theory.

---

## 1. Classical Textbooks and Academic References

* **Elements of Electromagnetics (7th Edition)**  
  *Author:* Matthew N. O. Sadiku  
  *Description:* Renowned for its clear pedagogical explanations, extensive numerical examples, and thorough treatment of vector calculus, Maxwell's equations, and transmission lines.

* **Introduction to Electrodynamics (4th Edition)**  
  *Author:* David J. Griffiths  
  *Description:* Authoritative undergraduate physics text celebrated for its deep conceptual insights into field theory, relativistic electrodynamics, and radiation mechanics.

* **Engineering Electromagnetics (9th Edition)**  
  *Authors:* William H. Hayt Jr., John A. Buck  
  *Description:* Practical engineering approach emphasizing transmission lines, waveguides, wave reflection, and boundary conditions.

* **Field and Wave Electromagnetics (2nd Edition)**  
  *Author:* David K. Cheng  
  *Description:* Rigorous mathematical exposition connecting electrostatic boundary value problems to electrodynamic radiating systems.

## 2. Online Courseware and Lecture Series

* **MIT OpenCourseWare: Electromagnetics and Applications (6.013)**  
  *Website:* [https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-013-electromagnetics-and-applications-spring-2009/](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-013-electromagnetics-and-applications-spring-2009/)  
  *Description:* Full lecture notes, assignments, and exam archives covering photonic devices, transmission line dynamics, and radiation.

* **Electromagnetism I & II (University of Colorado Boulder)**  
  *Website:* [https://www.colorado.edu/physics/](https://www.colorado.edu/physics/)  
  *Description:* Interactive simulations and PhET physics modules illustrating Coulomb forces, Faraday induction, and wave polarization.

---

## 3. Computational and Simulation Tools

* **MEEP (Finite-Difference Time-Domain Simulation Software)**  
  *Repository:* [https://github.com/NanoComp/meep](https://github.com/NanoComp/meep)  
  *Description:* Open-source FDTD simulation package for modeling electromagnetic wave propagation and photonic crystal structures.

* **PyFR & FEniCS (Finite Element Solvers)**  
  *Website:* [https://fenicsproject.org/](https://fenicsproject.org/)  
  *Description:* Python scientific computing frameworks for solving partial differential equations including Poisson's and Helmholtz's equations.
