# Recommended Resources: Logic Design

This document compiles curated textbooks, digital design suites, simulation tools, and academic course materials for digital systems and logic design.

---

## 1. Textbooks and Reference Manuals

* **Digital Design: With an Introduction to the Verilog HDL, VHDL, and SystemVerilog (6th Edition)**  
  *Authors:* M. Morris Mano, Michael D. Ciletti  
  *Description:* The premier standard international textbook for introductory digital logic, Boolean algebra, K-map minimization, MSI devices, and synchronous sequential circuits.

* **Fundamentals of Logic Design (7th Edition)**  
  *Authors:* Charles H. Roth Jr., Larry L. Kinney  
  *Description:* Renowned for pedagogical clarity in gate-level minimization, algebraic proofs, number systems, and timing hazard analysis.

* **Digital Electronics: Principles, Devices and Applications**  
  *Author:* Anil K. Maini  
  *Description:* Practical reference covering IC logic families (TTL 7400 series, CMOS 4000 series), propagation delays, fan-out, and noise margins.

---

## 2. Software Simulators & Design Environments

* **Logisim-evolution**  
  *Repository:* [https://github.com/logisim-evolution/logisim-evolution](https://github.com/logisim-evolution/logisim-evolution)  
  *Description:* Modern open-source graphical tool for designing and simulating digital logic circuits with subcircuit hierarchy, automated combinational synthesis, and VHDL export.

* **Digital (HNeemann)**  
  *Repository:* [https://github.com/hneemann/Digital](https://github.com/hneemann/Digital)  
  *Description:* Fast, lightweight digital circuit simulator designed for education, with integrated truth table analysis, automatic test vector execution, and hardware export.
