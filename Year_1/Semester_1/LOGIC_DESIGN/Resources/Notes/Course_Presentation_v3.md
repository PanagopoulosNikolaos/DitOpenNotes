# Course Presentation

This document presents the structure, objectives, and content of the "Digital Systems Design" course. It includes the weekly schedule of lectures and labs, student evaluation methods, and recommended bibliography for studying the subject.

---

## 1. Course Objectives and Requirements

Upon completion of the course, students will be able to:
- Understand the fundamental principles governing digital systems.
- Understand binary and other numbering systems, perform operations on them, and use them for information representation.
- Know Boolean algebra and apply it for implementing logical operations.
- Understand and combine basic logic units to create complex combinational digital circuits.

## 3. Evaluation Criteria

The final grade of the course is derived from:
- **70%** from the final written exam.
- **30%** from the written progress exam during the semester (valid only for the current exam period).
- **Laboratory:** Attendance is mandatory. Successful completion of 2 to 4 assignments is required (with "pass/fail" criteria). If an assignment fails, it must be repeated. A student who fails the laboratory is excluded from the final written exam.

## Solved Exercises

### Exercise 1: Final Grade Calculation
**Problem:**
A student scored $6.5/10$ on the final written exam and $8.0/10$ on the progress exam. Calculate the student's final grade, assuming they have successfully completed the laboratory.

**Solution:**
1. The final grade is derived from the weighted average of the final exam ($70\%$) and the progress exam ($30\%$).
2. Apply the formula:
   $$
   \text{Grade} = (0.70 \times \text{Exam\_Grade}) + (0.30 \times \text{Progress\_Grade})
   $$
3. Substitute the values:
   $$
   \text{Grade} = (0.70 \times 6.5) + (0.30 \times 8.0) = 4.55 + 2.40 = 6.95
   $$
4. The final grade is rounded to $7.0/10$.

## Exam Tip: Laboratory Prerequisite
In exams, many students forget that successful completion of the laboratory is a strict prerequisite for participating in the written exam. Ensure timely and successful submission of all laboratory assignments.
