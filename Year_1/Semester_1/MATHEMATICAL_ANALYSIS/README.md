# Mathematical Analysis

## Course Overview
This course delivers a rigorous grounding in the theoretical foundations and computational techniques of single-variable differential and integral calculus. Students master limits, continuity, differentiation theorems, curve sketching, optimization, Riemann integration, and introductory complex numbers.

## Course Code
101 (MATHEMATICAL ANALYSIS)

## Prerequisites
None (High school algebra, trigonometry, and precalculus)

---

## Topics Covered
* **Real Functions of a Real Variable**: Domain, range, injectivity, surjectivity, composition, inverse functions, exponential and logarithmic functions, trigonometric and inverse trigonometric functions.
* **Limits of Functions**: Intuitive and formal $(\varepsilon, \delta)$-definitions of limits, one-sided limits, infinite limits and limits at infinity, limit laws, squeeze theorem, indeterminate forms.
* **Continuity**: Continuity at a point and on an interval, classification of discontinuities, Intermediate Value Theorem (IVT), Extreme Value Theorem (EVT), Bolzano's theorem.
* **Differential Calculus**: Instantaneous rate of change, formal limit definition of derivatives, differentiability implies continuity, differentiation rules (product, quotient, chain rule), derivatives of transcendental functions, implicit differentiation, higher-order derivatives.
* **Theorems of Differential Calculus**: Rolle's Theorem, Mean Value Theorem (MVT), Cauchy's Mean Value Theorem, L'Hopital's Rule for indeterminate limits.
* **Applications of Derivatives**: Monotonicity intervals, local and global extrema, concavity, points of inflection, horizontal and vertical asymptotes, complete curve sketching, applied optimization modeling.
* **Integral Calculus**: Antiderivatives, indefinite integrals, integration techniques (substitution method, integration by parts, partial fractions, trigonometric integrals), Riemann sums, definite integrals.
* **Fundamental Theorem of Calculus**: Parts 1 and 2 of the FTC, differentiation of integral functions (Leibniz rule), computation of planar areas and arc lengths.
* **Complex Numbers**: Algebraic form, complex plane (Argand diagram), conjugate, modulus, polar and exponential forms (Euler's formula), De Moivre's theorem, roots of complex numbers.

---

## Learning Objectives
* Compute limits analytically using algebraic simplification, standard limits, and L'Hopital's rule.
* Prove properties of continuous functions using the Intermediate Value and Extreme Value theorems.
* Apply differentiation rules to complex algebraic and transcendental functions.
* Analyze and sketch functions using first and second derivative tests, determining extrema and asymptotes.
* Evaluate definite and indefinite integrals using substitution, parts, and partial fractions.
* Calculate geometric areas bounded by curves using definite integrals.
* Perform algebraic operations and root finding on complex numbers.
* Model and visualize calculus problems computationally using GNU Octave.
