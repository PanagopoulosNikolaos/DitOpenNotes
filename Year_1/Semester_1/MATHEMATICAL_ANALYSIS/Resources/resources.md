# Recommended Resources: Mathematical Analysis

This document compiles curated calculus textbooks, computational software packages, interactive mathematics tools, and academic course archives.

---

## 1. Textbooks and Reference Manuals

* **Calculus: Early Transcendentals (8th Edition)**  
  *Author:* James Stewart  
  *Description:* The primary undergraduate textbook for the course, known worldwide for mathematical rigor, visual clarity, practical engineering problems, and complete single-variable coverage.

* **Thomas' Calculus (14th Edition)**  
  *Authors:* Joel R. Hass, Christopher E. Heil, Maurice D. Weir  
  *Description:* Classic university reference covering the foundations of analysis, limits, proofs of the Mean Value Theorems, techniques of integration, and series.

* **Principles of Mathematical Analysis (3rd Edition)**  
  *Author:* Walter Rudin  
  *Description:* Renowned advanced theoretical benchmark ("Baby Rudin") for formal epsilon-delta proofs, metric spaces, and Riemann-Stieltjes integration.

---

## 2. Computational Software & Visualization Tools

* **GNU Octave (Symbolic and Numerical Calculus)**  
  *Website:* [https://octave.org/](https://octave.org/)  
  *Description:* Open-source numerical computing environment featuring high-precision adaptive quadrature algorithms (`integral`, `quad`) and 2D/3D curve plotting.

* **Desmos Graphing Calculator**  
  *Website:* [https://www.desmos.com/calculator](https://www.desmos.com/calculator)  
  *Description:* Real-time interactive browser grapher for visualizing parametric functions, secant/tangent line slopes, and Riemann sum approximations.

* **GeoGebra Math Suite**  
  *Website:* [https://www.geogebra.org/](https://www.geogebra.org/)  
  *Description:* Dynamic geometry and calculus visualization package illustrating solids of revolution, epsilon-delta neighborhoods, and inflection points.

---

## 3. Academic Video Lecture Archives

* **MIT 18.01: Single Variable Calculus (Prof. David Jerison)**  
  *Platform:* MIT OpenCourseWare  
  *Description:* Complete 35-lecture video course covering differentiation, applications to curve sketching, integration techniques, and fundamental theorems.

* **3Blue1Brown: Essence of Calculus**  
  *Website:* [https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/calculus](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/calculus)  
  *Description:* Visually intuitive animated series explaining the core geometric intuition behind derivatives, integrals, and the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus.
