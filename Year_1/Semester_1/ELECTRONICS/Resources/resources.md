# Recommended Resources: Electronics

This document compiles curated textbooks, simulation tools, online lecture courses, and interactive circuit design environments for undergraduate analog electronics.

---

## 1. Textbooks and Reference Manuals

* **Electronic Devices and Circuit Theory (11th Edition)**  
  *Authors:* Robert L. Boylestad, Louis Nashelsky  
  *Description:* The premier standard undergraduate reference covering semiconductor diodes, BJT and FET transistors, DC biasing, small-signal models, and power supply design.

* **Microelectronic Circuits (8th Edition)**  
  *Authors:* Adel S. Sedra, Kenneth C. Smith, Tony Chan Carusone, Vincent Gaudet  
  *Description:* Rigorous international benchmark for analog circuit analysis, integrated semiconductor device physics, and amplifier frequency response.

* **Engineering Circuit Analysis (9th Edition)**  
  *Authors:* William H. Hayt, Jack E. Kemmerly, Jamie Phillips, Steven M. Durbin  
  *Description:* Foundational textbook for resistive DC circuits, nodal/mesh analysis, Thevenin/Norton equivalents, and AC steady-state principles.

---

## 2. Simulation Toolchains & Design Suites

* **NGSPICE (Open Source Circuit Simulator)**  
  *Website:* [https://ngspice.sourceforge.io/](https://ngspice.sourceforge.io/)  
  *Description:* High-performance mixed-signal SPICE simulator for Linux command line, supporting `.op`, `.dc`, `.tran`, and `.ac` analyses.

* **LTspice (Analog Devices)**  
  *Website:* [https://www.analog.com/en/resources/design-tools-and-calculators/ltspice-simulator.html](https://www.analog.com/en/resources/design-tools-and-calculators/ltspice-simulator.html)  
  *Description:* Industry-standard schematic capture and SPICE simulation tool featuring built-in models for thousands of discrete semiconductor devices.

* **Falstad Circuit Simulator**  
  *Website:* [https://www.falstad.com/circuit/](https://www.falstad.com/circuit/)  
  *Description:* Interactive animated browser-based simulator illustrating real-time electron movement, voltage levels, and waveform shapes.

## 3. Reference Standards & Technical Data

* **JEDEC Solid State Technology Association**  
  *Website:* [https://www.jedec.org/](https://www.jedec.org/)  
  *Description:* Global standards for semiconductor device packaging, parameter testing, and reliability specifications.

* **Diodes Incorporated & ON Semiconductor Component Datasheets**  
  *Key Devices:* 1N4007 (1A Rectifier), 1N4148 (High-Speed Diode), 1N4736A (6.8V Zener), 2N2222A (NPN BJT), 2N3904 (General Purpose NPN).
